Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 28, 2026

Paramo ("Paramo," "we," "us," or "our") operates the Paramo SMS Alerts program (the "Program"), a text messaging service that sends trading research and system updates to individuals who opt in. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with the Program.

Information We Collect

When you opt in to the Program by texting the keyword PARAMO to +1 (408) 833-3360, we collect your mobile phone number and the timestamp and content of your opt-in message. We do not collect any other personal information through the Program unless you voluntarily provide it to us directly.

How We Use Your Information

We use your mobile phone number solely to send you SMS messages related to the Program, including trading research updates, system alerts, and program-related notices you have requested. We do not use your mobile information for any purpose other than the purpose for which consent was given.

Information Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as necessary to facilitate the Program itself — for example, our SMS platform provider (Twilio), acting solely to transmit messages on our behalf — or as required to comply with applicable law, regulation, or legal process. All other categories of information exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties for any purpose.

Data Retention and Security

We retain phone numbers and opt-in records for as long as necessary to operate the Program, respond to opt-out requests, and comply with legal and carrier requirements. We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect this information from unauthorized access.

Your Choices

You may revoke your consent to receive messages at any time by texting STOP to +1 (408) 833-3360. See the Terms & Conditions below for full opt-out and support instructions.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at paramotrading@outlook.com.
